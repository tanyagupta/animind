A little brown insect crawls laboriously from one corner of my cement heavy terrace to another. I wonder what thoughts it [thinks], what are its goals and aspirations, its dreams and disappointments, its friends and enemies.

I check my email, a friendly notice from the county pops up. “Vacuuming or squashing nymphs are the easiest way to reduce the population of spotted lanternfly, without using pesticide,” it said.
